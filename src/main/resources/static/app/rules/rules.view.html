Points System

Match Bets

You can update your bet for each match until its kickoff time. Results are based on 90 minutes only (no extra time or penalties).

Example: United States vs Mexico — you predict USA wins 3–1
  • Mexico wins or draw → 0 points
  • USA wins 2–0 → 1 point (correct result)
  • USA wins 3–1 → 3 points (result + exact score)

Virtual Coin Prize Pool

Instead of real money, we use virtual coins. Every action in the game adds coins to a shared prize pool (the “bucket”). The bigger the pool, the bigger the bragging rights!

You can see the live pool total in the navbar badge.

Tie-Breaking Rules
  • 2 players share 1st place — they split 80% (1st + 2nd prizes); the next-best score gets 20%.
  • 3+ players share 1st place — they split the entire 100%.
  • 1st place is unique, 2+ share 2nd — 1st gets 50%; tied players split 50% (2nd + 3rd).
